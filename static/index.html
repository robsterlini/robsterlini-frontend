Hi, I’m Rob Sterlini. I’ve been creating user first, typographically rich, and accessible web experiences since 2013.

Between nine and five

I joined UNiDAYS as a Staff Software Engineer in summer 2024. In that time I have led the frontend strategy for a new, best-in-class authentication and verification system with composability, accessibility and delivering a frictionless journey for our users at its core.

We extensively researched, prototyped and tested the nuances of right-to-left interfaces before building a system that worked with Arabic, delivering student verification for a trillion-dollar company as it opened a new marketplace in Saudi Arabia.

I mentor engineers of all experience levels, helping them grow their skills with a particular focus on frontend technologies like TypeScript, React and Next.js.

Between 2021–24, I re-imagined home decisions at Zoopla. As a Senior Software Engineer 1 in the Valuation Leads team, I worked on building novel journeys and enhancing existing experiences that connected prospective sellers to estate agents.

I then transitioned into the Web Platform team as a Senior Software Engineer 2 (Staff) where I focused on improving the core performance of zoopla.co.uk through CWV optimisation, as well as enabling product teams to deliver with robust tooling, infrastructure and technical guidance.

It was at Zoopla that I was part of a small but mighty team dedicated to championing accessibility in our products and processes; we were recognised by ContentSquare with a third place award for Best Digital Accessibility Mission.

Before that, I spent nearly eight years building web experiences at Fueled. I led projects for global giants like Apple, Pinterest, Vanity Fair, and Tony Robbins.

On the side, I’ve worked with Kickpush to develop their digital presence, created a PSA to help folks with pluralising LEGO (it’s not legos), and for my Dad’s 60th birthday I brought his pizza recipe to the masses.
