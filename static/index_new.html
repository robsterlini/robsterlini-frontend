Between nine and five

I have been a Staff Software Engineer at UNiDAYS since summer 2024. In that time I have led the frontend strategy and implementation for a new, best-in-class authentication and verification system with composability, accessibility and our users experiences at its core.

Within all of my past, present and future roles, I ensure that there is time to mentor and support other engineers as they learn and grow, specifically mentoring as a TypeScript SME.

Between 2021–24, I worked to re-imagine home decisions at Zoopla. Initially, as a Senior Software Engineer as part of the Valuation Leads team focused on products that connected prospective sellers to estate agents; before moving into the Web Platform team as a Staff Software Engineer where I focused on the foundations of zoopla.co.uk, improving performance through CWV optimisation, and enabling product teams.

It was here that I was part of a small team dedicated to accessibility improvements to our products and processes; we were recognised by ContentSquare with a third place award for Best Digital Accessibility Mission.

Before that, I spent nearly eight years building web experiences at Fueled. I led projects for the likes of Apple, Pinterest, Vanity Fair, and Tony Robbins.

On the side, I’ve worked with Kickpush to develop their digital presence, created a PSA to help folks with pluralising LEGO (it’s not legos), and for my Dad’s 60th birthday I brought his pizza recipe to the masses.
